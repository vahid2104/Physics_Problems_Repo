Problem 4 – Motion of a Particle in a Uniform Electric Field

Given Data

We have a charged particle moving in a uniform electric field.

m = 0.02 kg
q = 1 mC = 1 × 10⁻³ C
E = (30, 100) N/C
v(0) = (20, 0) m/s
r(0) = (0, 0)

1. Equations of Motion

Step 1: Electric force

The electric force on a charged particle is:

F = qE

Substitute the values:

F = (1 × 10⁻³)(30, 100)

So:

F = (0.03, 0.10) N

Step 2: Acceleration

Using Newton’s second law:

F = ma

So:

a = F / m

Substitute:

a = (0.03, 0.10) / 0.02
a = (1.5, 5) m/s²

Step 3: Velocity equations

Because acceleration is constant:

vx(t) = vx(0) + ax t
vy(t) = vy(0) + ay t

Substitute the values:

vx(t) = 20 + 1.5t
vy(t) = 5t

Therefore:

v(t) = (20 + 1.5t, 5t)

Step 4: Position equations

x(t) = x(0) + vx(0)t + ½axt²
y(t) = y(0) + vy(0)t + ½ayt²

Substitute the values:

x(t) = 20t + 0.75t²
y(t) = 2.5t²

Therefore:

r(t) = (20t + 0.75t², 2.5t²)

2. Animated Motion Trajectory

The particle starts at the origin. It moves to the right because its initial velocity is in the x-direction. It also curves upward because the electric field gives it upward acceleration.

x-position: 0.000 m
y-position: 0.000 m

vy: 0.000 m/s

3. Time to Reach Vertical Velocity of 50 m/s

The vertical velocity equation is:

vy(t) = 5t

We want:

vy = 50 m/s

So:

50 = 5t

Divide by 5:

t = 50 / 5
t = 10 s

4. Kinetic Energy After t = 0.05 s

The kinetic energy formula is:

K = ½mv²

where:

v² = vx² + vy²

At t = 0.05 s:

vx = 20 + 1.5(0.05) = 20.075 m/s
vy = 5(0.05) = 0.25 m/s

Now:

v² = (20.075)² + (0.25)²
v² = 403.068125

Therefore:

K = ½(0.02)(403.068125)
K = 4.03068125 J ≈ 4.03 J

Energy bar

This bar compares the initial kinetic energy with the kinetic energy after 0.05 s.

Initial K = 4.00 J, Final K = 4.03 J

5. Energy Balance Check

The work-energy theorem says:

ΔK = W

The work done by the electric field is:

W = qE · Δr

At t = 0.05 s:

x = 20(0.05) + 0.75(0.05)² = 1.001875 m
y = 2.5(0.05)² = 0.00625 m

Now calculate the dot product:

E · r = Ex x + Ey y
E · r = 30(1.001875) + 100(0.00625)
E · r = 30.68125

Then:

W = (1 × 10⁻³)(30.68125)
W = 0.03068125 J

The initial kinetic energy is:

K(0) = ½(0.02)(20)² = 4.00 J

The final kinetic energy is:

K(0.05) = 4.03068125 J

So:

ΔK = 4.03068125 - 4.00
ΔK = 0.03068125 J

Since:

ΔK = W
0.03068125 J = 0.03068125 J ✅

Therefore, the energy balance is correct.

Final Answers

  • Electric force: F = (0.03, 0.10) N
  • Acceleration: a = (1.5, 5) m/s²
  • Velocity: v(t) = (20 + 1.5t, 5t)
  • Position: r(t) = (20t + 0.75t², 2.5t²)
  • Time to reach vy = 50 m/s: t = 10 s
  • Kinetic energy at t = 0.05 s: K ≈ 4.03 J
  • Work done by field at t = 0.05 s: W = 0.03068125 J
  • Energy check: ΔK = W ✅
