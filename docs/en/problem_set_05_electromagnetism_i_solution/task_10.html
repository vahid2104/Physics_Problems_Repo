Problem 10 – Electric Field Flux

Verification of Gauss's Law using a sphere around a point charge

1. Definition of Electric Flux

Electric flux tells us how much electric field passes through a surface.

ΦE = ∮ E · dA

For a closed surface, the electric flux depends on the total charge inside the surface.

ΦE = q / ε0

This is called Gauss's Law.

2. Sphere Around a Point Charge

We place a point charge at the center of a spherical surface. The electric field points outward in every direction.

E = kq / R²

On a sphere, every point is the same distance from the charge. So the electric field magnitude is the same everywhere on the sphere.

A = 4πR²

Therefore, the total flux is:

ΦE = E A = (kq / R²)(4πR²)
ΦE = 4πkq = q / ε0

6. Step-by-Step Explanation

Step 1: Electric field from point charge

E = kq / R²

The electric field is stronger when the charge is larger and weaker when the radius is larger.

Step 2: Area of the sphere

A = 4πR²

The surface area increases when the radius increases.

Step 3: Flux through the sphere

Φ = EA

Substitute the formulas:

Φ = (kq / R²)(4πR²)

The R² cancels:

Φ = 4πkq

Step 4: Use k = 1 / 4πε0

Φ = q / ε0

This shows that the flux depends only on the charge inside the sphere, not on the radius of the sphere.

Important: changing the radius changes E and A separately, but their product stays the same.
