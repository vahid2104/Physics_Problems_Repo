Problem 2 – Velocity Selector

Crossed electric and magnetic fields can select particles with one special velocity. In this presentation, we show why the condition is v = E / B.

Given data

We are given crossed electric and magnetic fields:

E⃗ = (0, E, 0)
B⃗ = (0, 0, B)

This means:

  • The electric field points in the positive y-direction.
  • The magnetic field points in the positive z-direction.
  • The particle moves mainly in the x-direction.

The Lorentz force is:

F⃗ = q(E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗)

1. Condition for rectilinear motion

Rectilinear motion means straight-line motion. For the particle to move straight, the total force must be zero.

F⃗ = 0

Using the Lorentz force:

q(E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗) = 0

Since the charge is not zero, we divide by q:

E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗ = 0

Now assume the velocity is along the x-axis:

v⃗ = (v, 0, 0)

And the magnetic field is:

B⃗ = (0, 0, B)

Now calculate the cross product:

v⃗ × B⃗ = (v, 0, 0) × (0, 0, B)
v⃗ × B⃗ = (0, -vB, 0)

The electric field is:

E⃗ = (0, E, 0)

So:

E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗ = (0, E, 0) + (0, -vB, 0)
E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗ = (0, E - vB, 0)

For straight-line motion:

E - vB = 0

Therefore:

E = vB

Solving for velocity:

v = E / B

So the selected velocity is:

vd = E / B

2. Calculate selected velocity

We are given:

E = 400 V/m
B = 0.8 T

Use the formula:

vd = E / B

Substitute the values:

vd = 400 / 0.8

Calculate:

vd = 500 m/s

Therefore, the selected velocity is 500 m/s.

3. Does kinetic energy change in steady motion?

Kinetic energy is:

K = 1/2 mv²

In steady straight-line motion, the particle velocity is constant. That means the kinetic energy is also constant.

v = constant
K = constant

The net force is zero:

F⃗net = 0

Therefore, the net work is zero:

Wnet = 0

Using the work-energy theorem:

Wnet = ΔK
ΔK = 0

So the kinetic energy does not change in steady motion.

4. Operating principle of the velocity selector

A velocity selector allows only particles with a specific velocity to pass straight through. The electric and magnetic forces must cancel each other.

Too slow

If v < E/B, then magnetic force is weaker.

qvB < qE

The particle is deflected in the direction of electric force.

Correct speed

If v = E/B, the forces are balanced.

qvB = qE

The particle moves straight through.

Too fast

If v > E/B, then magnetic force is stronger.

qvB > qE

The particle is deflected in the opposite direction.

Case Condition Force result Particle motion
Too slow v < E/B FE > FB Deflects downward
Selected velocity v = E/B FE = FB Straight line
Too fast v > E/B FB > FE Deflects upward

Final answers

Condition for rectilinear motion

E⃗ + v⃗ × B⃗ = 0
vd = E / B

Numerical result

vd = 400 / 0.8 = 500 m/s

Kinetic energy

ΔK = 0

The kinetic energy does not change in steady motion.

Operating principle

The selector balances electric and magnetic forces. Only particles with velocity E/B pass straight through. Slower or faster particles are deflected.
